Hi! I'm **Szymon Tadeusz Ślązak**, also known as **Likisss** — a high school student who builds things that fly (or at least tries to).

Right now I'm a 4th-year mechatronics technician student in Opole. In my free time I like to read (you can also find some of my thoughts on the books I've read on this page), create stuff and wonder why that stuff doesn't work. I'm also part of the SatLab AGH team, which builds picosatellites.

As for my work life: I'm currently working as an electronics engineer at let's say an automotive company. To be more precise, I develop and test custom PCBs for food delivery vehicles. My role has also evolved a bit beyond electronics, and now I also help maintain and develop part of the software for those systems.

This blog will focus on things I find interesting: my projects, loose thoughts on books and space topics, and maybe some of my adventures and photos I've taken.

Currently, my main project is "Design and Laboratory Model of an X-Band Transceiver with Machine-Learning-Assisted FHSS Spread Spectrum for Inter-Satellite and Satellite-to-Ground Communication". It consists of:

- creating a PCB capable of transmitting and receiving in the X-band,
- creating an ML model for the FHSS algorithm,
- designing the FPGA code for the Altera Cyclone V module,
- building the whole laboratory model to test the ML and the idea itself.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to me through my social media.
